Investigating renewable generation and load dynamics across nine heterogeneous energy datasets using exploratory statistical diagnostics, ADF/KPSS stationarity testing, and LOESS seasonal decomposition (STL). FORECASTING & DEEP LEARNING
2024 – 2027 • DOCTORAL RESEARCH

Multi-Step Energy Time-Series Forecasting with Expanding-Window LSTM

Developing time-aware deep LSTM networks incorporating lookback sliding windows, time-respecting splits, and expanding-window cross-validation for short-term and medium-term load prediction. Benchmarked with substantial RMSE and MAPE error reductions across multiple horizons (1h to 24h).
