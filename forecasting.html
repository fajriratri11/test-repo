THEORETICAL FOUNDATION

Overview

Forecasting focuses on predicting future events, trends, and system behaviors using historical data, statistical methods, and machine learning techniques. In industrial and engineering systems, forecasting helps anticipate demand, resource needs, operational risks, and performance changes under uncertainty.

Prediction

Future Trend & Event Prediction

Projecting future trajectories and seasonal shifts based on historical time-series patterns.

Anticipation

Demand & Resource Anticipation

Estimating upcoming demand loads, raw material needs, and operational resource requirements.

Risk Evaluation

Operational Risk Assessment

Evaluating likelihoods of shortages, capacity bottlenecks, or critical threshold breaches.

Uncertainty

Decision Making Under Uncertainty

Providing reliable forecasts with confidence bounds to guide scheduling and resource allocation.
