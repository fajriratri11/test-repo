Selected Empirical Projects

Applying the FUSION methodology to renewable energy diagnostics, deep learning load forecasting, generation capacity planning, and enterprise decision tools.

Energy Systems Analytics

Exploratory Data Analysis for Renewable Solar, Wind, and Electricity Demand

Systematic empirical investigation across nine energy datasets using statistical diagnostics, ADF/KPSS stationarity testing, and LOESS seasonal decomposition (STL). Captures distinct predictability bounds between diurnal solar insolation and stochastic wind regimes.

Deep Learning Forecasting

Multi-Step Energy Time-Series Forecasting with Expanding-Window LSTM

Time-aware deep LSTM networks incorporating lookback sliding windows and expanding-window walk-forward validation for short- and medium-term load prediction. Achieves verified error reductions across multi-step predictive horizons with empirical confidence intervals.

Dynamic Characteristics of Renewable Energy Generation and Consumption Using LSTM Deep Learning Algorithm

M. Fahrudin Muna, Asif Iqbal Malik, Mujahid N. Syed
Results in Engineering, Volume 29, 2026, 103822. DOI: 10.1016/j.rineng.2026.103822
Abstract Summary: Examines how static and dynamic temporal variables shape renewable energy generation and consumption across nine datasets. The workflow combines exploratory statistical diagnostics, visualization, and STL decomposition to reveal multi-scale seasonal structure, then evaluates LSTM forecasting using time-aware preprocessing and expanding-window cross-validation.
