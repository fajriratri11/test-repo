2026 · Results in Engineering · Journal article

Dynamic Characteristics of Renewable Energy Generation and Consumption Using LSTM Deep Learning Algorithm

M. Fahrudin Muna, Asif Iqbal Malik, Mujahid N. Syed

Forecasting Data Analytics Decision Support
Abstract summary The study examines how static and dynamic temporal variables shape renewable energy generation and consumption across nine datasets, including regional consumption series, weather-linked demand, household smart-meter data, wind, and solar generation. The workflow combines exploratory statistical diagnostics, visualization, and STL decomposition to reveal multi-scale seasonal structure, then evaluates LSTM forecasting using time-aware preprocessing, sliding windows, and expanding-window cross-validation. The results show distinct temporal behaviors for solar, wind, household, and regional energy series, and the validation-enhanced LSTM setup improves performance over a non-validated configuration.
  • Best fit with the forecasting and analytics themes.
  • Useful as the lead publication for the energy-data storyline.
  • Supports the research direction toward intelligent decision support.

2025 · Engineering Headway · Survey article

A Survey of Sentiment Analysis in Halal Tourism Using Machine Learning and Lexicon Approaches for Extracting Sentiment Polarity

B. Jan, A. Hamid, M. F. Muna, H. Aljamaan

Data Analytics Machine Learning Decision Support
Abstract summary This survey reviews sentiment analysis research in halal tourism, a fast-growing sector spanning halal food, sharia hotels, banking, cosmetics, medicines, and finance. It synthesizes machine learning and lexicon-based approaches for extracting sentiment polarity from reviews and public opinion, including Random Forest, Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machine, Decision Tree, CNN, BERT, and attention-based models. The paper positions sentiment analysis as a practical tool for understanding tourist perceptions and guiding future research in halal tourism services.
  • Best fit with the data analytics theme.
  • Works well as a literature-entry publication on text analytics and opinion mining.
  • Can be positioned as decision intelligence for the service sector.

2023 · AIP Conference Proceedings · Conference paper

Usability Testing of Smart Inventory Software for EOQ, ROP and SS with Recommendation Decision in the Context of SMEs

M. Fahrudin Muna, Fitri Trapsilawati, Mirwan Ushada, Safira Alsana

Decision Support Optimization Usability
Abstract summary The paper describes a smart inventory software designed to help SMEs decide inventory quantity, economical order size, ordering time, and supplier selection. The system is implemented with a database-backed Microsoft Access and VBA workflow, while inventory planning uses EOQ and supplier selection relies on a minimum-function rule. Usability is evaluated with user testing, a within-subject design, thinking-aloud and interview techniques, plus objective measures such as completion time, error rate, and success rate. The reported SUS score improves from 65 to 76.8 after revision, indicating better usability in the second version.
  • Strong fit with decision support and intelligent optimization.
  • Good bridge between method design and user-centered evaluation.
  • Useful as a practical SME-oriented publication in the portfolio.

4
2022 · Universitas Gadjah Mada · Thesis

Pengembangan Dan Evaluasi Usabilitas Sistem Pendukung Keputusan Pada Proses Bisnis UMKM

M. Fahrudin Muna · supervised by Fitri Trapsilawati

Decision Support Optimization Usability
Abstract summary The thesis addresses inventory-management problems faced by SMEs under demand fluctuations and uncertain economic conditions, especially during the pandemic period. It develops a decision support system to estimate inventory needs, economical order quantities, ordering time, and supplier choice, using a database-based application implemented with Microsoft Access and VBA. The thesis also evaluates usability through user testing with seven respondents, combining thinking aloud, interviews, completion time, error rate, success rate, and System Usability Scale (SUS). The revised version performs better, with SUS improving from 65 to 76.8.
  • This is the thesis foundation for the inventory decision-support line of work.
  • It connects naturally to the optimization and decision support themes.
  • Pairs well with the conference paper that expands the same problem space.

2015 · IEEE 12th International Multi-Conference on Systems, Signals & Devices · Conference paper

Stiction Compensation Using PI-Sliding Mode Control for Pneumatic Valve

S. Elferik, M. F. Muna, T. B. Susilo, I. H. Imran

Simulation Optimization Control Systems
Abstract summary The paper focuses on oscillations in process control loops caused by valve stiction, a common nonlinear fault that can waste energy, increase variability, and degrade product quality. It proposes a compensation approach based on PI-sliding mode control to handle unknown stiction levels, reduce process variability, and extend valve life. Simulation results are used to demonstrate the method’s performance and compare it with recent published approaches, making the paper a strong fit for the modelling, simulation, and optimization themes.
  • Best fit with modelling and simulation, with a clear optimization/control angle.
  • Shows an earlier but still relevant systems-and-control research direction.
  • Complements the later decision-support and energy analytics work.
