Dynamic Characteristics of Renewable Energy Generation and Consumption Using LSTM Deep Learning Algorithm

M. Fahrudin Muna, Asif Iqbal Malik, Mujahid N. Syed
Results in Engineering, Volume 29, 2026, 103822. DOI: 10.1016/j.rineng.2026.103822
Abstract Summary: The study examines how static and dynamic temporal variables shape renewable energy generation and consumption across nine datasets, including regional consumption series, weather-linked demand, household smart-meter data, wind, and solar generation. The workflow combines exploratory statistical diagnostics, visualization, and STL decomposition to reveal multi-scale seasonal structure, then evaluates LSTM forecasting using time-aware preprocessing, sliding windows, and expanding-window cross-validation. The results show distinct temporal behaviors for solar, wind, household, and regional energy series, and the validation-enhanced LSTM setup improves performance over non-validated configurations by up to 27.4% in RMSE.

A Survey of Sentiment Analysis in Halal Tourism Using Machine Learning and Lexicon Approaches for Extracting Sentiment Polarity

B. Jan, A. Hamid, M. F. Muna, H. Aljamaan
Engineering Headway, 2025.
Abstract Summary: This comprehensive survey reviews sentiment analysis research in halal tourism, an expansive sector spanning halal food, sharia hospitality, banking, and cosmetics. It synthesizes machine learning and lexicon-based approaches for extracting sentiment polarity from customer reviews and public opinion, comparing Random Forest, Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machines, Decision Trees, CNNs, and attention-based Transformer models.

CONFERENCE PAPER • AIP PUBLISHING 2023

Usability Testing of Smart Inventory Software for EOQ, ROP and SS with Recommendation Decision in the Context of SMEs

M. Fahrudin Muna, Fitri Trapsilawati, Mirwan Ushada, Safira Alsana
AIP Conference Proceedings, Volume 2646, 2023.
Abstract Summary: The paper presents smart inventory management software engineered to assist small and medium enterprise decision-makers in determining order batching, reorder timing, buffer safety stock, and supplier selection. The system is implemented using a database-backed workflow, while inventory planning combines EOQ, ROP, and minimum-function supplier optimization. Formal usability engineering is conducted using a within-subject protocol, think-aloud interviews, completion timing, and the System Usability Scale (SUS), yielding a verified score improvement from 65.0 to 76.8.

MASTER'S THESIS • UNIVERSITAS GADJAH MADA 2022

Pengembangan Dan Evaluasi Usabilitas Sistem Pendukung Keputusan Pada Proses Bisnis UMKM

M. Fahrudin Muna • Supervised by Dr. Fitri Trapsilawati, S.T., M.Eng.
Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM), Yogyakarta, Indonesia, 2022.
Abstract Summary: The master's thesis addresses inventory-management bottlenecks faced by Indonesian SMEs under sharp macroeconomic and pandemic demand fluctuations. It formulates and implements a decision support system for estimating replenishment needs, economic order quantities, safety stocks, and multi-supplier allocation using a database-backed application. Usability was evaluated with seven representative respondents, demonstrating marked improvements in task completion error rates and raising SUS scores from 65 to 76.8.

CONFERENCE PROCEEDINGS • IEEE 2015

Stiction Compensation Using PI-Sliding Mode Control for Pneumatic Valve

S. Elferik, M. F. Muna, T. B. Susilo, I. H. Imran
IEEE 12th International Multi-Conference on Systems, Signals & Devices (SSD), 2015. DOI: 10.1109/SSD.2015.7348123
Abstract Summary: The paper addresses persistent limit-cycle oscillations in industrial process control loops caused by pneumatic valve stiction. It proposes a robust compensation methodology based on PI-sliding mode control to handle unknown friction bands, reduce loop process variability, and extend actuator service life. Simulation benchmarks demonstrate fast convergence to the invariant sliding manifold and complete elimination of stick-slip hunting.
